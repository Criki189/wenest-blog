# How much does a professional house cleaning service cost in Sydney in 2025?

It is 7:30 AM on a Tuesday. You have a 9 AM call, the kids' breakfast dishes are still in the sink, and you just noticed the hardwood floors look like a construction zone. You keep toying with the idea of hiring help, but you have absolutely no idea what a fair house cleaning service cost in Sydney actually looks like.

By the end of this, you will know the exact hourly rates and flat fees for every type of clean in 2025, plus what pushes the price up.

## Sydney house cleaning rates: the raw numbers

Let us get straight to the figures. Professional house cleaning prices in Sydney generally fall between $40 and $60 per hour per cleaner. 

That is not a fixed rule. An independent cleaner working off Gumtree might charge $35 an hour. A fully insured agency with police-checked staff will sit closer to $50 or $55. Then you have the extreme end — post-renovation cleans or heavy mould remediation — where quotes hit $70 or $80 an hour because the work is genuinely awful.

Most cleaning businesses enforce a minimum two-hour visit. If you only want someone for an hour to tidy a studio apartment, they will still bill you for two. 

Did a quote in Bondi for a two-bedroom unit last February. The owner had been quoted $45 an hour by an independent, but the job took the cleaner six hours because the bathroom grout had been ignored for years. An agency would have sent two cleaners for three hours at a slightly higher hourly rate, halving the disruption and finishing at the same total price. 

## Hourly rates versus flat fees

Cleaners structure their pricing in two ways: hourly or flat fee. 

Hourly suits regular maintenance. You book a cleaner for three hours every fortnight. They do what they can in that window. If the house is particularly messy one week, the vacuuming might get skipped. You pay for time, not outcomes.

Flat fees suit one-off events. A move-out clean, a spring clean, or an end-of-lease bond clean. Here you are paying for a checklist. The cleaner stays until the oven is degreased and the skirting boards are wiped. 

Ask three cleaners how much does a cleaner cost in Sydney for a flat-fee deep clean and you will get three completely different numbers. We have seen a three-bedroom house quoted at $280. We have seen the exact same house quoted at $550. The difference is usually how desperate the cleaner is for work that week, and whether they actually plan to clean inside the kitchen cabinets or just wipe the doors.

## What actually drives the price up

Look, what actually matters is the scope of the job and the condition of the home. After that, it is just size. The rest is noise.

A few specific things will inflate your quote rapidly:

**Size and layout:** A four-bedroom, two-bathroom house in the Sutherland Shire takes twice as long to clean as a two-bedder in Surry Hills. Add a second storey and you are paying for another hour of labour just from the stairs and transiting between floors.

**Pets:** Pet hair on soft furnishings adds serious time. If you have a shedding golden retriever, expect a $10 to $20 surcharge per visit or an extra hour on the quote. It gets into the carpet, the couch, and the air conditioning filters.

**Cleaning products and equipment:** Some independents ask you to supply the chemicals, vacuum, and mop. This lowers their rate but means you are storing the gear. Agencies bring their own commercial-grade, often eco-friendly supplies. You pay a premium for that convenience — usually built into the higher hourly rate.

**Frequency:** Fortnightly cleans are cheaper per visit than monthly ones. Monthly cleans are cheaper than one-off deep cleans. A house that gets wiped down every two weeks never reaches the grime stage that takes three hours to scrub away.

## Deep cleans, spring cleans, and move-out cleans

Standard cleans cover the visible surfaces. Benches, floors, bathrooms, kitchens, dusting. Deep cleans go into the mechanics of the house. 

A proper spring clean includes wiping down skirting boards, cleaning inside the oven, scrubbing the shower grout with a dedicated brush, and wiping the tops of cupboards. In Sydney, expect a flat rate of $300 to $500 for a three-bedroom house.

Move-out or bond cleans are a different beast entirely. Real estate agents in Sydney are notoriously strict about end-of-lease cleanliness. You need the carpets steam cleaned, the blinds dusted, and the oven spotless, or they will dock your bond. 

These jobs usually start around $400 for a small apartment and easily clear $700 for a large family home. If you need carpet steam cleaning on top of the general clean, add roughly $30 to $40 per room. 

Honestly nobody quotes this consistently — we have seen $180 and we have seen $420 for the exact same bond clean job in the Inner West. It depends entirely on the agency's relationship with the property manager and whether they guarantee their work against the agent's inspection.

## When to hire a professional versus doing it yourself

If you have the time, basic weekly maintenance is entirely doable yourself. Wiping benches, vacuuming high-traffic areas, and keeping the bathroom relatively mould-free takes about 90 minutes a week if you stay on top of it.

The cheapest quote is almost always the most expensive job. A $35-an-hour cleaner who breaks your Dyson or uses bleach on your timber floors will cost you thousands in repairs. 

Hire a professional service when:
- You are moving out and need to protect your rental bond.
- You are preparing to sell your home and need it pristine for photography.
- You have neglected the bathrooms for six months and the grout is turning black.
- Your time is genuinely worth more than the cleaning fee.

If you are spending your Saturday morning cleaning when you could be working or with your family, the maths is usually pretty clear.
